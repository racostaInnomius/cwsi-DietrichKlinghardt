Getting Stripe ready to take real payments

The site has three separate places that collect money, and each one needs something different from your side before it can go live. This page is a checklist you can work through with your team — tick items off as you complete them.

Foundation Donations

Powers the giving form at /foundation/donate

Donations route through Beytrax's Stripe platform, then attribute straight to the Foundation's own connected account — so the Foundation gets the funds, but never has to touch Stripe's checkout code itself. Until that account is fully verified, the donation page stays in a friendly "opening soon" state. Nothing breaks — it also just doesn't collect yet.

What needs to be ready

How to get there

  1. Go to dashboard.stripe.com and create an account (or sign in to an existing one) under the Foundation's legal name.

  2. Under Settings → Business settings, fill in the legal entity name, EIN or tax ID (if the Foundation has one), registered address, and the name and email of a responsible person.

  3. Under Settings → Bank accounts and scheduling, add the account that should receive donation payouts.

  4. Check your status: on the Stripe Home page, look for "You can now accept payments." You can also confirm under Settings → Account status.

  5. Once that's done, Beytrax will send a secure Stripe connection link — click it and approve the connection. That's the step that actually links your account to the donation page.

Track 2 of 3

Store Checkout

Powers purchases at /store

Store purchases use Dr. Klinghardt's own Stripe account directly, not Beytrax's. Beytrax only needs a limited-permission key to open a checkout page on your behalf — your full account access, and every dollar, stays with you.

What needs to be ready

How to get there

  1. Sign in to (or create) the Stripe account that should receive store payments, and confirm payments are enabled the same way as above.

  2. Go to Developers → API keys → Create restricted key. Name it something recognizable, like "Beytrax Store Integration."

  3. Under permissions, set Checkout Sessions to Write, and leave every other permission at None.

  4. Click Create key and copy it immediately — Stripe only shows it once. Send it to Beytrax through a private channel (a password manager's share link works well). Never by plain email, text, or chat.

  5. Send Beytrax your final product list — name, price, a short description, and a photo for each — so they can load it in for you to review before it goes live.

Track 3 of 3

Payment Links

Powers Events, Courses, and the Weekly Talks membership

These don't run through a checkout on the site at all — a visitor is sent straight to a Stripe-hosted payment page that you create yourself, one per event, course, or membership tier.

What needs to be ready

How to get there

  1. In your Stripe Dashboard, check the toggle in the top-right corner and make sure it reads Live — links made in test mode can't take a real payment.

  2. Go to Payment Links → + New and create one link for each thing you're selling this way: every individual event, every course, and the Weekly Talks membership.

  3. Copy each link's address (it starts with buy.stripe.com/…) and send Beytrax the full list, clearly labeled with which event, course, or membership each one belongs to.

  4. Before sending, check each link's price against the price written on the site page — these live in two separate places, and nothing keeps them in sync automatically.

!

Quick rule of thumb: anything Stripe calls a Secret key, or shows you only once, should never travel by plain email, text, or chat. If you're not sure how to share something safely, ask Beytrax first — it takes two minutes and avoids a much bigger cleanup later.
